# Prompt used for ChatGPT

Act as a professional Software Quality Assurance engineer. Generate at least 15 test cases for this requirement:

"The ExamPoint University Online Exam Registration Module shall allow students to enter personal details, select one or more available examination subjects, and pay the applicable examination fee. The system shall validate mandatory fields, verify selected subjects, calculate the total fee according to the fee rules, and confirm registration only after successful payment. If details are invalid or payment fails, it shall display an appropriate message and must not mark registration as successfully completed."

For every test case include: ID, scenario, preconditions, test input or steps, expected result, and category. Include positive, negative, boundary-value, and exception cases. Cover valid registration, missing fields, invalid email, subject selection, fee calculation, successful and failed payments, duplicate submission, unavailable subject, payment timeout, and unexpected system errors. Do not invent unspecified field limits, maximum subject counts, or fee amounts. Mark assumptions or requirements that need clarification. Avoid duplicate and irrelevant cases. Present the output in a structured table suitable for a student practical record.
